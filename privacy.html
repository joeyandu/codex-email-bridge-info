Privacy policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026.

Purpose and data used

Codex Email Bridge is a personal desktop utility. It uses the authorized Google account's email identity to verify the configured sender and uses Gmail sending permission to deliver task notifications to the owner's configured recipient.

The utility processes local Codex task names, final responses, questions, options and links. These are notification content supplied by local task events, not messages read from Gmail. No Gmail read, search or deletion permission is requested.

Storage and transmission

Google OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored in the owner's macOS Keychain. The desktop OAuth client configuration, outgoing event records and delivery logs are stored locally, outside the utility's synced project directory. The program itself does not upload these credential files or delivery records to a separate server.

Notification content is transmitted to Google through the Gmail API for delivery to the configured recipient. Copies may remain in the sender's and recipient's mailboxes according to their Gmail settings. This static information website does not receive the notifications or OAuth credentials.

AI and other uses

The utility may send local task names to the owner's existing Codex service to generate shorter notification titles. Google OAuth credentials and Google account identity are not included in those title prompts. The utility does not use Google user data for advertising, sale, profiling or training generalized AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Local outgoing event records are retained until the owner removes them. They support delivery review and duplicate prevention. Logs and local backups may also contain task identifiers or notification previews. Gmail retains sent and received messages under the account's own settings.

The owner can stop the background service and revoke the app's authorization from Google Account settings. Revoking access does not itself delete local records or already-delivered email. The owner can separately remove this app's Keychain credential, local data and mailbox copies.

This website and contact

These static pages use no analytics scripts, cookies, sign-in forms or trackers. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request data, such as IP addresses, under its own policy. Questions can be raised in the information repository's issue tracker; do not post passwords, tokens or private notification contents.

Contact through the project issue tracker.
